Life in every landscape

One planet.
Many habitats.

From tropical rainforests and open savannas to coral reefs and polar ice, habitats provide the food, water, shelter, and space wildlife needs to survive.

🌎 3 essentials

Food: energy to grow and live.

Shelter: protection from weather and danger.

Space: room to find resources, raise young, and move safely.

A future for wildlife

Small actions can make a difference.

Habitat loss, pollution, climate change, and illegal wildlife trade threaten species around the world. Supporting responsible conservation, reducing waste, and respecting wild places can help protect biodiversity.
